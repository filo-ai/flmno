The Courts is a playful tennis resort with the hills of the Anza-Borrego Desert as a panoramic backdrop. Full of tennis courts, a pool, and some overnight camper vans, the Courts invites the public to enjoy a casually bohemian holiday. Recently acquired by a young design couple, the Courts is a charming, inclusive scene awash in color that vibrates amidst the sandy environment. Taking inspiration from the vintage tennis vibe, the poster calls attention to booking a stay with puns and a book of matches.
