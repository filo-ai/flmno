Strike a Match

A tennis oasis in a California desert

Client
The Courts
Year
2021
Skills & Tools
Illustration, poster, promotion

The Courts is a playful tennis resort with the hills of the Anza-Borrego Desert as a panoramic backdrop. Full of tennis courts, a pool, and some overnight camper vans, the Courts invites the public to enjoy a casually bohemian holiday.

Impact

Cultural Traction

The poster campaign generated organic sharing and became a recognisable visual reference in the local arts community.

Event Attendance

The illustrated materials contributed to a marked increase in first-time attendees who discovered the event through the poster.

Collector Demand

Limited print runs sold out ahead of the event, with requests for additional copies continuing afterwards.
