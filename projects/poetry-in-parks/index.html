Poetry in Parks

Ada Limón, the 24th U.S. Poet Laureate, graced the mountain with her presence and her project: You Are Here. Her project focused on a dedicated poem and a picnic table to commemorate the event.

Impact

Literature in the Landscape

The project introduced poetry as a mode of interpretation, giving visitors a slower, more reflective way to experience the park.

Educator Adoption

Local schools used the postcards as prompts in creative writing programmes, extending the work into classrooms.

Unexpected Audience

The series reached readers who had no prior connection to the park, brought in through a shared love of poetry.
