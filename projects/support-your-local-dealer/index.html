Case Study

Support Your Local Dealer

A droll promotional poster for a Denver family-owned art shop

Client
H. R. Meininger Co*
Year
2020
Skills & Tools
poster, advertising, illustration, *

Finding that lots of students and art dilettantes end up going to Amazon or big box stores for their art supplies, Meininger's invited several artists to advertise their shop.

Impact

Community Response

The campaign resonated strongly with independent business communities, generating press coverage and grassroots sharing.

Tonal Precision

The humorous framing made a serious economic argument feel approachable and shareable without losing its point.

Visual Longevity

The posters remain in use at supported businesses years after the initial campaign, a sign of genuine community adoption.
