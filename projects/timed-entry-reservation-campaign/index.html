To support the launch of a new timed entry reservation system at Mount Rainier National Park, I developed a cohesive public service announcement campaign aimed at educating visitors before arrival and reducing congestion at park entrances, trails, and sites. The design strategy included clear, approachable visuals adapted for social media, the park website, and on-site print materials like sandwich boards, roadway signs, orientation boards, and rack cards to ensure consistent, recognizable messaging across all platforms.
