Impact

Trail Adoption

Download and print numbers for the maps exceeded projections in the first season, indicating genuine visitor need.

Ranger feedback confirmed the maps reflected current trail conditions accurately — a rare achievement in rapidly-changing terrain.

Pocket-Sized Utility

The compact format was repeatedly praised by hikers who had struggled with full-size park maps in the field.
