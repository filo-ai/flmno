The task was simple: the CSO wasn't attracting a cool, young crowd. Enter: astrology. Wait — no: astronomy. The project takes the star charts of traditional sky maps into consideration and the placement of instruments on the stage, the posters and collateral design helps elevate the terrestrial orchestra into something celestial. With an orchestra that performs euphonic pieces, the youth is sure to be in awe struck with the CSO's stellar musicians. Or at the least, put their stellar merch within reach. *This project is conceptual.
