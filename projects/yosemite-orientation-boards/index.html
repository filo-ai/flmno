Impact

First-Hour Confidence

Visitors reported feeling oriented and ready to explore within minutes of arriving — a direct result of the board design.

Directional enquiries to rangers dropped noticeably after the new boards were installed, freeing staff for deeper visitor engagement.

Bilingual Reach

The visual-first layout meant the boards worked effectively for international visitors regardless of language.
