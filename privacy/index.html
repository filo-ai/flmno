Privacy

This is the portfolio site of Filo Merid (flmno), based in Seattle, WA. Here is what happens to information on it.

The contact form

If you use the contact form, I collect the name, email address and message you type in. They are emailed to me through the email service Resend and used only to reply to you. I don’t sell or share them, and I don’t add you to any mailing list.

Analytics

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It doesn’t use cookies and doesn’t track you across other websites.

Embedded content

Some project pages may show posts embedded from Instagram. Those embeds load content from Instagram, which can set its own cookies under its own privacy policy.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operation.

Your choices

To ask what I have from you, or to have it deleted, send a message through the contact form.
